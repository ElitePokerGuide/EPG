# EPG voice

Who we are on screen: the friend who has watched every poker course so you don't have to. Not a guru, not a hype man. A sharp study partner who respects your time.

## Tone
- Direct, calm, confident. We state the idea, then the reason. No filler.
- Specific over generic. "Bet 33% on A72 rainbow" beats "bet small on dry boards".
- Teach the *why*. Every claim comes with the reasoning a coach would give.
- Respect the viewer. They are not stupid, they are busy. No "most players are idiots" energy.
- Dry humor is fine. Sarcasm about the viewer is not.
- Honest about uncertainty. When schools disagree, we say so. That is our edge, not a weakness.

## Language rules (EN)
- Plain English, short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Poker terms are fine (c-bet, OOP, range, polarized) but the first 3 seconds of a short must be understandable to an NL5 player.
- No "Exciting news", "Let's dive in", "Game changer", "Unlock", "Crush", "Secrets". Ever.
- Numbers only when they carry meaning. Round them (33%, 2/3 pot, ~70%).
- Address the viewer as "you". We are "we" (the EPG team), never "I".

## Hooks (first 1.5 seconds)
- Lead with the mistake, the contradiction, or the question. Never with "In this video".
- Pattern bank lives in `hooks.yaml`; weights are updated by `pipeline/measure`.

## CTA
- Educational first: "Save this for your next session", "Which line would you take? Comment."
- Funnel second, soft, max once per post: "Full breakdown + the free study sheet: link in bio."
- Never: "Buy", "Course", "Discount", "Sale", school names, trainer names, room names, bonuses.

## Visual identity (for render/)
- Dark felt palette from the EPG logo, one accent colour, one display font, one body font.
- Our own hand replayer. Never screenshots of other software or courses.
- Captions: word-level, high contrast, max 4 words per line.
